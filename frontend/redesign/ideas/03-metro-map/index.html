    04 Why save every version

    Overwrite once and it’s gone.

    Without versions

    song_final.midsong_final2.midsong_final_REAL.midsong_final_v3_USE_THIS.mid

    With STAVE

    • Undo anything

      Bring back the chord frame from two weeks ago without losing today’s melody.

    • Try bold ideas

      Branch off, rewrite the bridge, and merge it only if you love it.

    • See who changed what

      Each station keeps the author, the time and the exact notes.

    05 How it works

    Draft, commit, branch, merge.

    1. 1 · Draft

      Sketch freely in the editor. Nothing is written to history yet.

    2. 2 · Commit

      Happy with it? Name the moment and it becomes a station on the line.

    3. 3 · Branch

      Open a new line to try an idea without touching main.

    4. 4 · Merge

      Bring the line back. Where two lines meet is an interchange.
