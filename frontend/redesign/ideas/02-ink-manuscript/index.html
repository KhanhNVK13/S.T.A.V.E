    № 03 / 07

    undo forgets · ink remembers

    Why keep every version?

    1. i.
      Be brave with ideas. Try the strange chord. The safe take is still on the previous page.
    2. ii.
      See exactly what changed. Compare two takes note by note, not by ear alone.
    3. iii.
      Go back to any day. Last Tuesday's groove is one click away, forever.

    № 04 / 07

    four pages, one song

    Draft, commit, branch, merge.

    1. p. 1

      Draft

      Sketch freely in the editor. Nothing is final until you say so.

    2. p. 2

      Commit

      Save a named snapshot — a take you can always return to.

    3. p. 3

      Branch

      Try another idea on its own staff, without touching the main line.

    4. p. 4

      Merge

      Bring the best ideas back together, bar by bar.
