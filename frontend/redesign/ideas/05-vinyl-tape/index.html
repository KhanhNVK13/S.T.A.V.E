Why keep every version

The best take is rarely the last one.

  • A1

    Nothing is ever lost

    Rewind to any earlier take with one click. Your old ideas stay exactly as you left them.

  • A2

    Try bold ideas safely

    Split off a branch, go wild, and keep the main take untouched until you are sure.

  • B1

    See exactly what changed

    Blue for added notes, dashed pink for removed, amber for new velocity. No guessing.

  • B2

    Make music together

    Everyone works on their own copy. Merge when ready, and nobody records over anybody.

Tools

A full studio rack behind the history.

  • 128 instruments

    The full General MIDI palette, from pianos and strings to synths and effects.

  • Drums on their own lane

    Kick, snare and hats get a dedicated pad grid instead of a pitch ladder.

  • Your own sounds

    Bring a sample and map it to a key. It travels with the project history.

  • Take it anywhere

    Export .mid for your DAW, or bounce WAV and MP3 to share.
