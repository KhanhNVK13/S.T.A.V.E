Why keep every version

Never lose a good idea again.

  • Go back to any moment. That first chorus you deleted at 2 a.m. is one click away.
  • Try bold ideas safely. Experiment on a branch. The original never moves.
  • See who changed what. Every note edit is credited to the person who made it.

    How it works

    From first sketch to finished song in four moves.

    1. 01

      Draft

      Write freely in the editor. Nothing is permanent yet, so nothing can go wrong.

    2. 02

      Commit

      Save a named snapshot of the whole project whenever it sounds right.

    3. 03

      Branch

      Try a new bridge or drum feel on a separate line, without touching the original.

    4. 04

      Merge

      Bring the best branch home. STAVE shows every difference before it lands.
