Instrument sample credits
==========================

The instrument sound samples bundled under this directory (frontend/public/instruments/)
are pre-rendered per-note MP3 files from the FluidR3_GM SoundFont, sourced from:

  https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts

License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC-BY 3.0)
Original SoundFont author: S. Christian Collins (GeneralUser GS project)
  https://schristiancollins.com/generaluser.php

These files are used as-is (no modification beyond selecting which instruments/notes
to bundle) for Tone.Sampler-based playback and audio export in the MIDI Editor
(see frontend/lib/instrument-samples.ts, frontend/lib/tone-synth-engine.ts).
