Instrument sample credits
==========================

Melodic instruments (every folder except generaluser_drums/)
------------------------------------------------------------
The melodic instrument samples bundled under this directory
(frontend/public/instruments/) are pre-rendered per-note MP3 files from the
FluidR3_GM SoundFont, sourced from:

  https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts

License (as distributed by that repository): Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0)
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Original SoundFont: FluidR3_GM by Frank Wen (Copyright 2000-2002, 2008 Frank Wen;
2008 Toby Smithe), originally released under the MIT license — per the Debian
fluid-soundfont package copyright file:
  https://metadata.ftp-master.debian.org/changelogs/main/f/fluid-soundfont/unstable_copyright

These files are used as-is (no modification beyond selecting which instruments/notes
to bundle) for Tone.Sampler-based playback and audio export in the MIDI Editor
(see frontend/lib/instrument-samples.ts, frontend/lib/tone-synth-engine.ts).

Drum kits (generaluser_drums/)
------------------------------
The "Standard Drum Kit" and "Orchestra Drum Kit" use the samples and zone data of
the "Standard 1" (bank 128, program 0) and "Orchestral" (bank 128, program 48)
drum kits of:

  GeneralUser GS 2.0.3 BETA by S. Christian Collins (Copyright 1997-2025)
  https://schristiancollins.com/generaluser.php

Taken from the compressed copy (GeneralUserGS.sf3) bundled with SpessaSynth
(https://github.com/spessasus/SpessaSynth, soundfonts/GeneralUserGS.sf3).
frontend/scripts/extract-generaluser-drums.mjs copies each sample the two kits use
out of that file as its original Ogg Vorbis stream (no re-encoding) and writes the
kits' zone parameters to generaluser_drums/kits.json; this repackaging is permitted
by the license below ("modify the SoundFont bank or its packaging to suit your needs").
